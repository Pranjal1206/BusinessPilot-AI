Problem Statement

Introduction

Managing invoices manually can be time-consuming, especially for small businesses, freelancers and independent professionals.

Problems We Identified

- Manually entering invoice details takes time.
- Important information may be missed while reading invoices.
- Tracking paid and unpaid invoices can become confusing.
- Calculating totals and pending amounts manually may lead to errors.
- Managing multiple invoices in different files makes organisation difficult.

Our Objective

Our objective is to develop an AI-powered Invoice Assistant that makes invoice management simpler, faster and more organised.

The proposed system will use AI to help extract important invoice details, generate summaries and assist users in tracking their invoice information.

Expected Outcome

We aim to create a user-friendly platform that reduces manual effort, improves organisation and makes invoice management easier for individuals and small businesses.
