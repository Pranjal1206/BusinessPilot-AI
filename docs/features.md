Project Features

Our AI Invoice Assistant is designed to make invoice management easier and more organised.

1. Invoice Upload

Users will be able to upload their invoice files to the platform.

2. AI-Based Data Extraction

The system will aim to identify important details such as:

- Invoice number
- Customer name
- Invoice date
- Due date
- Total amount

3. Invoice Summary

AI will help generate a simple summary of the uploaded invoice so users can understand its important information quickly.

4. Payment Tracking

Users will be able to organise invoices based on their payment status, such as paid, unpaid or pending.

5. Dashboard

A simple dashboard will help users view their invoice information in one place.

6. PayPal Integration

We plan to explore PayPal integration to make payment-related workflows easier.

Future Improvements

- Automatic reminders for due invoices
- Monthly invoice reports
- Better invoice search and filtering
- Support for multiple invoice formats

Note: These features represent the planned functionality of the project. They will be implemented and tested during development.
